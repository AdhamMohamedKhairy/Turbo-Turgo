The Project
WHAT IS TURBO TURGO?
The Turbo Turgo system is a micro-hydroelectric power generation device that uses a pressurized nozzle to shoot a high-velocity jet of water onto a specially-designed Turgo impulse turbine. The kinetic energy of the water jet is converted directly into rotational mechanical energy, which drives a generator to produce clean electricity.

Unlike conventional reaction turbines, the Turgo turbine allows the water jet to enter one side of the runner and exit the other, enabling higher flow rates and making it ideal for medium-head, high-flow water sources.
01
Water Source
Water is collected and fed into a pressurized pipe system, building up potential energy through elevation head.
02
Nozzle Injection
A precision-engineered nozzle converts water pressure into a high-velocity jet aimed at the turbine runner blades.
03
Turgo Turbine
The inclined cup-shaped blades of the Turgo runner convert water jet kinetic energy into rotational shaft power.
04
Power Generation
The spinning shaft drives a generator, producing usable AC or DC electricity with minimal conversion losses.

Our Mission
WHY TURBO TURGO?

Access to electricity remains a challenge in many remote areas of the world. Conventional grid infrastructure is expensive to deploy, and large-scale hydroelectric projects require massive environmental interventions.

The Turbo Turgo project demonstrates that small-scale hydroelectric generation — using nothing more than a controlled water source, a precision nozzle, and an impulse turbine — can produce meaningful amounts of clean electricity for off-grid communities.

Our goal is to engineer a proof-of-concept system that achieves high efficiency, low cost, and easy replicability — so that anyone with access to a water source can generate their own power.
